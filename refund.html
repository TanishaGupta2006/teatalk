REFUND POLICY



At Talk & Tea Café, customer satisfaction is important to us. We prepare all food and beverages fresh to maintain quality and hygiene

  • Once an order is prepared and served, refunds or exchanges are not permitted.
  • If you receive an incorrect item or if there is a quality issue noticed at the time of service, please inform our staff immediately.
  • In genuine cases of error from our side, we may offer a replacement or store credit, subject to verification.

No refunds will be provided for:

  • Change of mind after ordering
  • Partially consumed food or beverages
  • Delays caused by high customer volume or peak hours

All refund or replacement decisions are at the sole discretion of Talk & Tea Café management.
